## Projects

### Post-Quantum Blockchain for Railway Signalling · *Jun 2026 – now*
Blockchain-based security architecture for railway signalling, using lattice-based cryptography for quantum-resistant authentication, secure communication and data integrity.

### Neural-SLAM-Zero · *Jan 2026 – now*
Self-supervised graph-transformer module that corrects SLAM drift, targeting a >40% reduction in Absolute Trajectory Error. Uses DINOv2 embeddings for loop-closure confidence with under 15 ms overhead on a Jetson Orin Nano. Funded under the IFPS scheme at SSN.

### Multilingual AI-Driven CDSS for Rural Healthcare · *Nov 2025*
Hybrid BioBERT and Bi-LSTM clinical decision support for neonatal sepsis, marasmus and tuberculosis, with multi-language support. Presented at ISCS 2025 (Delhi) and published in IEEE Xplore. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISCS69371.2025.11385867)

### AI-Driven Patient Outcome Enhancement · *Mar 2025*
CDSS for status asthmaticus and diabetic ketoacidosis with 90% severity-prediction accuracy and a React interface. **Best Paper** at the Next Gen International Conference (SRM).

### U2R Attack Detection in Wireless Networks · *Apr 2024*
CNN AlexNet model with higher U2R attack-detection precision than SVM baselines. Published at ACROSET 2024. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACROSET62108.2024.10743609)
